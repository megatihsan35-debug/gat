The model

A ring that rises to its two main gates

  • The roof is one wave. It is low at the Tengah and Timur gates (+10.200, two floors) and rises to +16.200 at the Selatan and Utara gates (three floors). So the main gate is framed by the two tallest ends of the building.
  • The third floor exists only under the high parts. It holds the musolla, the gallery and the meeting rooms, which keeps the building from being bigger than the brief needs.
  • The landmark is a slender mast through the roof opening at the exact centre, with a light at +30.000. That is about twice the height of the roof.
  • The façade is a screen of 205 vertical fins on the upper floors. They lie almost flat along the wall and turn open as they reach a gate, so the building looks solid from the side and transparent where you enter.

The façade

The whole elevation, opened out flat

Three ways to dress the upper floors, drawn on the same stretch of the west block from the low Tengah gate (left) to the high Utara gate (right). I recommend A.

A · Fins that open at the gates recommended, shown in the model

Strongest futuristic look and the best shade for the east and west sun. One repeated part, so it is simple to model in SketchUp. Changes as you walk past it.

B · Songket veil

Ties the building to local craft. Flatter and calmer than the fins. Needs a good pattern to avoid looking like a generic screen.

C · Ribbons

Closest to your “simpul” idea, and the bands could run out along the spines as canopies. Hardest to build and to draw, and it shades the low sun less well.

What the panel said

Still answered

1 · “If the concept is titik temu, why two radial areas? Make one.”

One centre. The public gets the ground floor and the court. The private rooms (admin, staff, musolla) are upstairs in the same ring. The qibla line passes through that centre and the musolla sits on it, now on the top floor.

2 · “Make the entrance concave. Use convex on the first floor so it hangs.”

At each gate the ground-floor wall is scooped back, 5.0 m deep at the main gate. The two upper floors keep the convex line and pass over it. Columns stay on one ring, so the real cantilever is 3.5 m, a third of the 10.5 m span behind it.

Plans

Ground floor on the wider site

  • The four spines keep the real bearings to each region: Selatan 183°, Tengah 243°, Utara 318°, Timur 28°. They run 37, 63, 41 and 41 m from the boundary to the centre.
  • The main forecourt rises 0.9 m over 21.2 m (about 1:24), so there are no steps and no separate ramp. Check against MS 1184 at plan stage.
  • Parking from the brief: 5 staff bays, 1 OKU bay and 10 motorcycles in the south-east corner, 10 bicycles by the arrival garden, a drop-off lay-by at the main gate.
  • The Tengah spine is 63 m long and crosses the arrival garden. On market days the Pekan Sehari and car-boot stalls line it, which the brief names as users.
  • The ring bridge is the corridor on both upper floors. On the second floor it runs under the funnel roof and links the two high parts.
  • Two cores (stairs and lifts) stand beside the two high gates, opposite each other across the court, and reach all three floors.
  • The musolla is on the top floor, centred on the qibla radial 292.3°, with 4.5 m of height under the rising roof.
  • The gallery shares the top floor with an open river deck that looks east over Jalan Ibrahim towards Sungai Pahang.
  • The funnel roof still sends rain through the Ø3 m opening into the pool at the centre.

Before SketchUp

Five things to check

  1. The new east boundary. I traced it from the brief’s site plan, where one pixel is about half a metre, and took it to the edge of Jalan Ibrahim. Please confirm the real line with your lecturer. If the land stops at the tennis court fence, the site is slightly smaller but the building still fits.
  2. Which façade: A, B or C?
  3. The high ends at Selatan and Utara. Are those the right two gates to make tall? The other choice is all four blocks rising the same way round, like a pinwheel.
  4. Back of house on Jalan Ibrahim. It is the shortest service route, but that side also faces the river. If you want the river side fully public, service goes to the south-east corner instead.
  5. The mast at +30.000 and the open decks (1,217 m²). I could not find a height limit in the brief. Tell me if either feels too much.
